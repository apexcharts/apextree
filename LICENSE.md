## 📄 License Options for ApexTree

ApexTree is a commercial product, included in the **Pro** plan and above.

**There is no free tier for ApexTree.** The $2M threshold that makes some Apex products free does not apply to this one: a Pro license is required whatever your financial resources, by companies, individuals, non-profits and educators alike. Without a valid license key ApexTree renders with a trial watermark, which is for evaluation only.

One license key works across every Apex product, so a customer of one does not buy a second for another.

---

### 💼 Commercial License (Paid)

ApexTree requires a **Pro** license or higher. Plans below Pro do not include it, and neither does the free Community License.

Pro and Premium are priced per developer, and priced on the features you use rather than on your size.

✅ What's included:

- Use in internal tools and commercial applications
- Modifications and app-level distribution
- 12-month subscription with updates & support

🚫 Not allowed:

- Redistribution in toolkits, SDKs, or platforms
- Use by unlicensed developers
- Competing charting or diagramming products

### 🏛 Non-Profit Tiers

Non-Profits are treated like commercial entities and need a **Pro** license or higher for ApexTree, whatever their annual operating budget. Discounts are available: ask us before you buy.

### 🔄 OEM / Redistribution License (Paid)

Required if you are **embedding ApexTree into a product or platform used by other people**, such as:

- No-code dashboards
- Developer platforms
- Embedded BI tools
- SDKs

✅ What's included:

- Redistribution rights for **one application or product**
- **Unlimited developer seats** on that application, with no separate per-developer license to buy
- **Every licensed feature**, at the highest tier
- 12-month subscription with updates & support

Your end users need no license of their own. A second application needs a second OEM license. White-label, resale, and affiliate arrangements are quoted separately.

✅ OEM **not required** if your app simply renders static diagrams and users **cannot** configure or interact with them.

---

### ⏳ When a subscription ends

Your license key expires and **ApexTree renders with a trial watermark**, because the whole product is licensed rather than a set of features inside a free one. You no longer receive updates, new features, or technical support. Your existing deployment keeps running.

For OEM licenses, continued redistribution requires an active subscription.
